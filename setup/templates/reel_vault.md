# Reel & share vault

Tips rescued from Instagram / YouTube / WhatsApp so they stay usable —
not buried in saved posts.

Drop forwarded text into `data/share_inbox/*.txt`, or say **“save this reel”**.

Or use the widget **Reels** panel / `POST /api/reels` with a public Instagram URL or a short video file
(stored under `data/reels/`). Ask Voxy: **“what was that reel about?”**
