# Owner's Knowledge Log

Voxoryl stores what you tell it here — one heading per topic, facts and asks listed underneath.
New topics create new headings automatically.

On first run, Voxoryl reads `setup/voxoryl.setup.json` and creates your private `data/knowledge.md`
from `setup/templates/knowledge.md`. Your real log stays under `data/` (gitignored).

Example shape only:

## Bikes

- **YYYY-MM-DD** — Owner likes bikes.
- **YYYY-MM-DD** — Owner owns a motorcycle.
