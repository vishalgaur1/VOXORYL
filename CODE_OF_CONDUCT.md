# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

Be respectful. VOXORYL is a small open project — we expect kindness, good faith, and technical honesty.

## Standards

**Do**
- Critique ideas, not people
- Assume positive intent
- Keep discussions on-topic (product, code, docs)

**Don’t**
- Harassment, hate, or personal attacks
- Spam, scrape private data, or share secrets
- Pressure maintainers for commercial access without a license

## Enforcement

Report problems to **vishalgaur2002@gmail.com**. Maintainers may warn, close threads, or ban accounts that break this.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) — kept short on purpose.
